## How to work here
- One folder per product under `products/<slug>/` with a `README.md` covering:
  audience, promise, price, sales channel, status (idea → building → listed →
  selling), and links to the listing.
- Track launch/revenue notes in `docs/STATUS.md` and decisions in
  `docs/DECISIONS.md` so the next chat can pick up where this one left off.
- Only use assets we own or are licensed for commercial use; note the license
  source in the product README.
- Ask the owner before spending money, publishing a listing, or changing prices.
